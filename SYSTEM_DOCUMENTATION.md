System Features
1. Appointment Booking System
Allows customers to book salon appointments online by selecting a service, choosing a stylist, and picking an available date and time.
2. Service Management
Enables the administrator to add, edit, update, and delete salon services, including prices, categories, variants, and service images.
4. Admin Dashboard
Provides an overview of important salon data such as appointments, customers, services, revenue, and booking status summaries.
5. Customer Booking Management
Lets customers view booking details, manage their appointments, and check confirmations or receipts.
6. Sales and Revenue Monitoring
Tracks completed services and records business income, helping the salon monitor daily and overall revenue.
7. Ratings and Feedback System
Allows customers to submit reviews and ratings, which helps evaluate service quality and customer satisfaction.
8. Role-Based Authentication
Controls access for different users such as admin, manager and customer, ensuring secure and proper use of the system.
9. Notification and Confirmation System
Sends or displays booking confirmations, status updates, and other important appointment-related notices.
